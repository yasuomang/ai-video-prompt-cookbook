# Lighting and Style Wording

Put lighting and style in the `Style:` field. Keep it short: one lighting phrase plus one look is usually enough. Long style lists make the output drift or look over-produced.

## Lighting

| Lighting | Feel | Prompt Wording |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Golden hour | Warm, soft, lifestyle | `golden hour backlight, warm rim light` |
| Overcast | Even, muted, calm | `soft overcast light, muted colors` |
| Hard sun | Contrast, summer, energy | `hard noon sun, deep shadows` |
| Low-key | Moody, dramatic | `low-key lighting, single practical lamp` |
| Neon night | Urban, nightlife, tech | `neon signage, wet street reflections` |
| Studio softbox | Clean product shots | `studio softbox lighting, seamless white background` |
| High-key | Bright commercial, ecommerce | `bright high-key commercial lighting` |
| Window light | Natural home or desk scenes | `soft morning window light` |
| Tungsten | Warm interiors, food | `warm tungsten light, dark background` |

## Looks and Styles

- `realistic ecommerce product video`: default for product clips.
- `clean UGC product clip, casual, not cinematic`: for creator-style ads.
- `clean premium commercial`: higher-end product spots.
- `documentary realism, handheld`: behind-the-scenes and real-world settings.
- `cinematic, shallow depth of field`: story or brand mood pieces.
- `shot on 16mm film, visible grain`: nostalgic, textured.
- `2D hand-drawn anime style` / `3D animated, soft stylized characters` / `stop-motion clay animation`: stylized content.

## Matching Style to the Clip Job

| Clip Job | Lighting | Style |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ecommerce product ad | studio softbox or high-key | realistic ecommerce product video |
| Skincare / beauty | soft morning window light | clean, warm, minimal |
| Food | warm tungsten | appetizing close-up, realistic |
| UGC hook | natural room light | casual, not cinematic |
| Launch teaser | low-key or neon | polished, minimal, modern |
| Brand mood film | golden hour | cinematic, shallow depth of field |

## Common Fixes

- Output looks like a trailer when you needed an ad: remove `cinematic` and add the platform (`vertical social ad`).
- Style changes partway through the clip: repeat the main style phrase at the end of the prompt.
- Colors don't match the product photo: add `keep original colors from the source image`.
