# Vertical Pet Hook Clip

Use this for scroll-stopping openers where the first second carries the clip.

```text
Subject: a golden retriever wearing sunglasses.
Camera movement: close-up, slight push-in.
Motion description: the dog turns its head toward the camera in slow motion as confetti falls.
Style: bright, saturated, playful.
Duration: 4 seconds.
Aspect ratio: 9:16.
Preservation constraints: keep the dog's face and sunglasses consistent.
Negative constraints: no extra animals, no text, no confetti covering the face.
Evaluation notes: check whether the motion starts in the first second and the face stays clean.
```

## Tip

Hook clips should be short. Generate 4 to 5 seconds and cut to the best 2 in editing.
