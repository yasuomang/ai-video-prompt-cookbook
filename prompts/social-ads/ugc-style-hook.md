# UGC-Style Hook Clip

Use this for creator-style ad concepts without implying a real endorsement.

```text
Subject: [product or app category] shown in a casual creator setup.
Camera movement: handheld but stable, slight push-in.
Motion description: quick reveal of the product or interface, natural desk or room movement.
Style: authentic creator video, simple lighting, social-first framing.
Duration: 5 seconds.
Aspect ratio: 9:16.
Preservation constraints: keep product/interface visible and consistent.
Negative constraints: no fake celebrity, no false testimonial, no unreadable text, no exaggerated claims.
Evaluation notes: check hook clarity, first-second attention, and whether the clip leaves room for captions.
```

## Caption Space

Leave top or bottom space for captions if the video will be edited for TikTok, Reels, or Shorts.
