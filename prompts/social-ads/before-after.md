# Before / After Transformation Clip

```text
Subject: a messy bedroom.
Camera movement: wide, locked off.
Motion description: objects fly into place and the messy bedroom becomes a tidy, minimalist bedroom.
Style: daylight, clean, realistic.
Duration: 5 seconds.
Aspect ratio: 9:16.
Preservation constraints: keep the room layout, window, and bed position the same before and after.
Negative constraints: no room changing shape, no people, no text.
Evaluation notes: check that the room is still recognizably the same space at the end.
```

## Tip

Use a start and end frame (real before and after photos) when the result has to match a real service or product.
