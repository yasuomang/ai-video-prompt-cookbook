# Photo Comes Alive Clip

```text
Subject: [an old family photo / group photo].
Input type: start frame / photo.
Camera movement: static.
Motion description: the people begin to move naturally: they smile, blink, and lean slightly closer together. The background stays still.
Style: keep the original photo's colors and grain.
Duration: 5 seconds.
Aspect ratio: match the source photo.
Preservation constraints: keep every face, outfit, and the photo's composition unchanged.
Negative constraints: no new people, no face changes, no colorizing unless asked.
Evaluation notes: check identity preservation for every face and whether the motion stays subtle.
```

## Safety Note

Only animate photos you have the right to use, and be careful with images of real people.
