# Launch Teaser Clip

Use this for abstract launch visuals when the final product UI or footage is not ready.

```text
Subject: abstract visual metaphor for [product promise or category].
Camera movement: slow reveal from dark foreground to clean product-colored background.
Motion description: light sweep and subtle particle-free movement, no distracting elements.
Style: polished launch teaser, minimal, modern, high clarity.
Duration: 5 seconds.
Aspect ratio: 16:9.
Preservation constraints: keep central negative space for logo or headline overlay.
Negative constraints: no unreadable text, no fake UI, no excessive cinematic clutter.
Evaluation notes: check if the clip can support a clear launch headline in editing.
```

## Best Use

Use as a background layer, not as the full message. Add real headline and product proof in editing.
