# Talking-to-Camera Creator Clip

Use this for creator-style framing tests. Don't use it to imply a real person endorses a product.

```text
Subject: a young man sitting in front of a ring light in a cozy room.
Camera movement: medium close-up, static, eye level.
Motion description: he talks to the camera with expressive hand gestures and laughs.
Style: soft ring-light catchlights, warm background bokeh, casual.
Duration: 5 seconds.
Aspect ratio: 9:16.
Preservation constraints: keep the face, hair, and clothing consistent.
Negative constraints: no fake celebrity, no false testimonial, no warped hands, no on-screen text.
Evaluation notes: check mouth movement, hand anatomy, and whether there is room for captions.
```

## Audio Note

For models with native sound, add a short spoken line in quotes, for example `He says: "Okay, this actually works."`, and keep it to one sentence.
