# Cinematic Scene From a Still Image

Use this for concept clips, mood tests, travel visuals, and cinematic background shots.

```text
Subject: [scene visible in the start frame].
Input type: start frame / cinematic image.
Camera movement: gentle orbit from left to right.
Motion description: subtle environmental motion such as wind, water, fabric, or light movement.
Style: cinematic, natural depth of field, realistic lighting.
Duration: [5-8 seconds].
Aspect ratio: [16:9].
Preservation constraints: keep subject placement and core scene composition stable.
Negative constraints: no sudden camera shake, no subject morphing, no new main subjects.
Evaluation notes: check motion coherence, scene stability, camera smoothness, and editability.
```

## Variation

For a calmer shot, replace camera movement with:

```text
Camera movement: locked camera, subtle parallax only.
```
