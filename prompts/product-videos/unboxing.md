# Unboxing Product Video

```text
Subject: a matte white box containing [smartwatch] nested in foam.
Input type: text-to-video or start frame of the closed box.
Camera movement: overhead, locked off.
Motion description: two hands slowly lift the lid, revealing the product inside.
Style: soft diffused light, minimal, clean shadows.
Duration: 5 seconds.
Aspect ratio: 1:1 or 9:16.
Preservation constraints: keep the box proportions and product shape consistent once revealed.
Negative constraints: no product appearing before the lid opens, no extra hands, no text on the box.
Evaluation notes: check that the reveal happens in the first 3 seconds and the product is fully visible at the end.
```

## Tip

For an exact product reveal, use an end frame showing the open box with the real product.
