# Lifestyle Desk Product Video

```text
Subject: [wireless earbuds case] on a wooden desk next to a laptop and a coffee mug.
Input type: text-to-video or start frame.
Camera movement: top-down close-up, very slight handheld movement.
Motion description: a hand picks up the case, opens it, and takes out one earbud.
Style: soft morning window light, warm neutral tones, cozy home office.
Duration: 5 seconds.
Aspect ratio: 9:16.
Preservation constraints: keep the case shape, color, and lid hinge realistic.
Negative constraints: no extra hands, no warped fingers, no duplicated earbuds, no visible brand text changes.
Evaluation notes: check hand anatomy, how the lid opens, and whether the product stays the visual focus.
```

## Tip

Hands are the main failure point. Keep it to one hand and one simple action.
