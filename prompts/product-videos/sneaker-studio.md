# Sneaker Studio Product Video

```text
Subject: a red sneaker on a matte white studio platform.
Input type: start frame / product image.
Camera movement: slow orbit from front-left to front-right.
Motion description: soft studio light moves across the sneaker, platform remains stable.
Style: clean footwear product ad, realistic, sharp silhouette.
Duration: 5 seconds.
Aspect ratio: 1:1.
Preservation constraints: keep the shoe shape, sole, laces, color, and logo placement unchanged.
Negative constraints: no extra shoes, no foot inside the shoe, no distorted laces, no text changes.
Evaluation notes: check shape stability, lace artifacts, camera smoothness, and product recognizability.
```

## Variation

For social ads, change aspect ratio to `9:16` and use `centered vertical framing`.
