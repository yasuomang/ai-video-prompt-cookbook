# Ingredient Splash Product Video

```text
Subject: a bottle of [juice / drink] surrounded by fresh orange slices.
Input type: start frame / product image.
Camera movement: static, macro framing.
Motion description: orange slices and water droplets burst outward around the bottle in slow motion, then settle.
Style: bright high-key studio light, vivid colors, slow-motion feel.
Duration: 5 seconds.
Aspect ratio: 9:16.
Preservation constraints: keep the bottle, label, and cap fixed in the center.
Negative constraints: no liquid covering the label, no extra bottles, no fruit merging into the bottle.
Evaluation notes: check label readability during the splash and whether the motion looks like slow motion rather than floating.
```

## Variation

Swap ingredients to match the product: coffee beans for coffee, mint leaves for gum or toothpaste, petals for fragrance.
