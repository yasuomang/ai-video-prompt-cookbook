# Text-to-Video Product Concept

Use this when no source image is available and the goal is an early creative direction.

```text
Subject: [product category] shown in a clean [environment].
Camera movement: slow push-in, centered product composition.
Motion description: product remains stable while light and background elements move subtly.
Style: premium product ad, realistic, minimal, high clarity.
Duration: [5 seconds].
Aspect ratio: [9:16].
Preservation constraints: keep product simple, recognizable, and consistent.
Negative constraints: no unreadable text, no extra hands, no distorted logo, no cluttered background.
Evaluation notes: judge concept direction, not final brand fidelity.
```

## When to Use

This works best for early mood testing. For production product clips, use image-to-video with a real product reference instead.
