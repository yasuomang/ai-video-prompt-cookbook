# Explainer Background Clip

Use this for subtle background visuals behind captions, product overlays, or educational narration.

```text
Subject: abstract visual metaphor for [topic].
Camera movement: slow forward movement through the scene.
Motion description: gentle motion, low visual complexity, no fast cuts.
Style: clean explainer background, modern, readable, non-distracting.
Duration: [6-8 seconds].
Aspect ratio: [16:9 / 9:16].
Preservation constraints: keep negative space for text overlays.
Negative constraints: no readable fake text, no busy foreground, no sudden scene change.
Evaluation notes: check whether captions or UI overlays would remain readable.
```

## Tip

If the clip competes with the narration or text overlay, reduce motion and contrast.
